family's white X and dark rounded-square base, then adds Motion's cyan keyframe
curve badge. Do not recolor or redraw the X; use the cyan `#3ad9f2` accent for
Motion surfaces.

asset. Regenerate it from the SVG without changing the canvas, crop, or colors.
