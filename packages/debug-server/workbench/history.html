Receipts history

Every Motion operation writes a durable receipt whose inputs and outputs are recorded as SHA-256 hashes. Receipts are not cryptographically signed — the hashes prove an artifact still matches what the operation recorded, not who wrote the file. This is the regression trail: what ran, when, who or what triggered it, whether the quality gates passed, and — critically — where the output artifacts landed on disk. When an agent drives Motion, this is where you confirm what it actually did.
